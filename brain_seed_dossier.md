## 1. Executive Summary & System Overview

**Brain** is an autonomous multi-agent predictive simulation engine developed by Obrive Industries. It enables enterprise leaders, quantitative strategists, and policy planners to test critical decisions against hundreds of interacting AI agent personas before deploying capital or launching initiatives in the real world.

### The Five Engine Pipeline:
1. **Scenario Definition & Ingestion**: Ingests unstructured reality seed documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD) and extracts entity ontologies, causal links, and environmental boundaries.
2. **Temporal Knowledge Graph Construction**: Powered by Zep Memory Graph, establishing dynamic relationship hierarchies, temporal episodes, and semantic vectors.
3. **OASIS Swarm Agent Synthesis**: Synthesizes heterogeneous agent personas with distinct demographics, ideological biases, cognitive styles, professional motivations, and emotional triggers.
4. **Multi-Platform Multi-Round Simulation**: Simulates organic social, economic, and debate interactions across simulated platforms (Twitter/X feeds, Reddit threads, professional councils) over simulated time horizons.
5. **Synthesis & Deep Refinement**: Generates comprehensive predictive intelligence reports with confidence intervals, consensus shifts, and direct multi-agent dialogue probes.

## 2. Core Strategic Dilemma for Simulation

### Scenario Name:
> **"Project Horizon: Commercial Launch & Go-To-Market Trajectory of the Brain Swarm Engine"**

### The Business Challenge:
Obrive is launching Brain to global enterprise and developer markets. The executive board is divided between two contrasting commercial strategies:

* **Strategy Alpha — The Enterprise Airgapped Model ($75,000/year/seat)**:
  - Target: Fortune 500 C-Suite, quantitative hedge funds, defense agencies, and top-tier management consultancies (McKinsey, BCG).
  - Value Prop: Zero-data-retention, dedicated on-prem/VPC deployment, customized enterprise swarm ontologies, and direct API access to private fine-tuned weights.
  - Risk: Long 6-to-9 month enterprise sales cycles, high customer acquisition cost (CAC), and risk of being overshadowed by rapid open-source developer movements.

* **Strategy Beta — The Open-Developer BYOK Freemium Protocol ($0 Platform Tier + Token-Based BYOK)**:
  - Target: AI engineers, startup founders, policy researchers, quantitative retail analysts, and university labs.
  - Value Prop: Instant onboarding, Bring-Your-Own-Key (Groq, OpenRouter, NVIDIA NIM, Google Gemini, OpenAI), zero platform markups, viral community-shared scenario libraries.
  - Risk: Lower immediate upfront contract values, potential server compute strain from free tiers, and risk of competitors reverse-engineering prompt pipelines.

* **Strategy Gamma — The Dual-Engine Hybrid Strategy**:
  - Community self-serve BYOK for viral distribution + Enterprise Tier for proprietary corporate data vaults, custom Zep graph retention, and compliance certification.

## 4. Key Environmental Constraints & Friction Points

1. **LLM Provider Cost & Latency Elasticity**:
   - Groq provides ultra-high throughput (~800 tokens/sec) enabling 100-round simulations in minutes.
   - High-reasoning models (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, DeepSeek R1) provide superior strategic depth but increase per-round latency.
2. **Zep Graph Density & Semantic Convergence**:
   - Knowledge graphs must maintain temporal provenance so agents remember past actions and update beliefs sequentially rather than resetting each round.
3. **The "Synthetic Astroturfing" Public Perception Risk**:
   - If public discourse misinterprets Brain as an automated bot farm rather than an analytical predictive testbed, brand backlash could occur.
